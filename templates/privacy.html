Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 26, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide when you create an account (such as your email address and password) and the expense records you enter into Spendly (such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions). We do not collect payment information.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Spendly service — displaying your expense history, generating summaries, and enabling filtering. We do not use your financial data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app for you.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We apply industry-standard measures including encrypted connections and access controls. You may delete your account at any time, which will permanently remove all associated data from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. We may use limited third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) who are bound by confidentiality obligations and may not use your data for their own purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at privacy@spendly.app. We aim to respond to all inquiries within 5 business days.
